## Placement Process

1. Start a student with **Year 1 + New** only if they have no previous placement.
2. Keep the student in their current year with **Continued** when they pass and stay in the same year.
3. Use **Promoted** with exactly the next year after the student passes. Example: **Year 1 + Promoted** saves the student as Year 2 for the selected next period.
4. Use **Retake** at the current year when any required course grade is failing.
5. Wait until all approved, non-dropped course grades are released before changing placement. Select the student checkbox and save the placement.
6. Promotion updates the student's stored year level. The student is then enrolled in course sections for that new year; do not create a second student account or count them twice.

The portal treats final grades from 1.00 through 3.00 as passing, unless the course status is `Fail`. A `Drop` course is excluded from the placement pass check.
